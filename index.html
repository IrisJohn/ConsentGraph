What is ConsentGraph?

🎯 Mission

ConsentGraph is the first framework to empirically measure DPDP Act 2023 compliance and detect dark patterns at scale. It analyzes websites for manipulative consent mechanisms, tracking technologies, and hidden data broker relationships.

🌍 Focus

Built for India's 900 million internet users, this research framework provides evidence for privacy regulation enforcement and helps understand the tracking ecosystem that affects user privacy.

About ConsentGraph

ConsentGraph is a research framework and Chrome extension that analyzes privacy compliance and detects dark patterns across websites. Built with a focus on India's DPDP Act 2023, it provides the first empirical evidence of systemic privacy violations affecting 900+ million internet users.
